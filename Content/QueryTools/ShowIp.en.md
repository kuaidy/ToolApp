## IP Geolocation Lookup

Look up IP address location, ISP, and geographic info—or view your current public IP. On local/private networks, your egress public address may be queried automatically.

### How to use

1. Enter an IP, or leave blank to query your current public IP
2. Start the lookup
3. Review location, ISP, and related fields

### FAQ

**Is it accurate to street level?**  
Results are usually city- or region-level estimates—not suitable for navigation or precise positioning.
