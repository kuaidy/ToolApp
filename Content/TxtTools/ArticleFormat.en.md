## Chinese–English Typography

Automatically normalize spacing and punctuation in mixed Chinese–English text for cleaner layout—useful for blogs, articles, and draft documentation.

### How to use

1. Paste the original text
2. Run one-click formatting
3. Compare and copy the formatted result

### FAQ

**Will it change my meaning?**  
It mainly adjusts whitespace and punctuation rules; it does not rewrite or polish content.

**What text works best?**  
Mixed Chinese–English body text, titles, and short documents.
