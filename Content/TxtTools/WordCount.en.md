## Word Count

Count Chinese and English words, characters, lines, and paragraphs online. Paste or type text for instant statistics—no software install required.

### How to use

1. Paste or type text in the input area
2. View live statistics on the right or on the page
3. Note the counts for submissions, translation, or reporting

### FAQ

**How are Chinese and English counted?**  
Chinese is counted by character; English by word, along with character, line, and paragraph metrics.

**Is my text uploaded?**  
Counting runs on the page; do not paste highly confidential content in untrusted environments.
