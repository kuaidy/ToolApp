## Encode / Decode

Encode and decode Base64, URL, HTML, and more online—for development, link parameters, and text transport.

### How to use

1. Choose an encoding type
2. Paste plain text or encoded text
3. Run encode or decode and copy the result

### FAQ

**Is Base64 supported?**  
Yes, along with common URL and HTML encode/decode options on the page.

**Is my content stored?**  
Processing happens in the page/session; do not use real secrets or key material here.
