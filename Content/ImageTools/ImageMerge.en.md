## Image Merge

Combine multiple images horizontally, vertically, or in a grid into one image. Preview and export in one step—ideal for long screenshots, comparisons, and tutorial step images.

### How to use

1. Upload multiple images in the desired order
2. Choose horizontal, vertical, or grid layout
3. Preview the stitch; adjust parameters if needed
4. Download the merged image

### FAQ

**How many images can I merge?**  
Limited by device memory and canvas size; keep count and resolution reasonable to avoid browser slowdown.

**Can I change the order?**  
Upload in the desired order, or reorder in the list before merging.

**What is the difference between horizontal and vertical?**  
Horizontal places images side by side; vertical stacks them—pick the layout that fits your content.
