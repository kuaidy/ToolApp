## Image HD Upscale

Upscale and enhance small or slightly blurry images for display, printing, or further editing. Improves appearance but cannot recover detail that was never in the source.

### How to use

1. Upload the source image
2. Choose an upscale factor
3. Process and preview
4. Download the enhanced result

### FAQ

**Can a very blurry image become sharp?**  
Appearance can improve, but lost information cannot be restored; clearer sources work better.

**What upscale factor should I use?**  
Try 2× first; very high factors may introduce artifacts.

**Is it suitable for printing?**  
Helpful for mild upscaling; poster-quality output still needs a higher-resolution original.
