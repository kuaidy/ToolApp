## Image Resize

Resize image width and height in the browser. Keep aspect ratio, use percentage presets, and export PNG, JPEG, or WebP. **Images are not uploaded to the server**, suitable for privacy-sensitive content.

### How to use

1. Select a local image to upload
2. Enter target width and height, or click a percentage preset
3. Enable “Keep aspect ratio” to avoid distortion
4. Apply the resize and download the result

### FAQ

**Is the image uploaded to the server?**  
No. Scaling and export are done in the browser via Canvas.

**Can I lock the aspect ratio?**  
Yes. Enable keep aspect ratio to prevent stretching.

**What is the maximum size supported?**  
Limited by browser and device memory; very large images may need to be reduced first.
