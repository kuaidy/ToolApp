## Image Format Conversion

Convert images locally in the browser to PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, or ICO—**no server upload**. ICO export can include multiple sizes from 16–256 px, suitable for images with private content.

### How to use

1. Select a local image
2. Choose the output format (optionally enable white background fill)
3. Start conversion and preview
4. Download the converted file

### FAQ

**Are images uploaded to the server?**  
No. Reading, conversion, and download all happen locally in the browser.

**Which output formats are supported?**  
PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, and ICO. ICO includes 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px sizes.

**How is transparency handled?**  
JPEG, WebP, and BMP do not support transparency; you can fill with white before export. PNG and ICO can keep transparency.
