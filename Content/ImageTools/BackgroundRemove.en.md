## Background Removal

Remove image backgrounds online: quick cutout, color-based removal, and AI-assisted modes. Preview and download transparent PNGs for e-commerce product shots and collage assets.

### How to use

1. Upload the image to process
2. Choose a removal mode and adjust tolerance or related settings as needed
3. Preview the transparent result
4. Download the transparent PNG

### FAQ

**What images work best?**  
Product shots and portraits with a clear subject and relatively plain background work well. For complex backgrounds, adjust tolerance or try another mode.

**How is privacy handled?**  
Quick and color-based modes run mainly in the browser. AI mode may use server-side inference—do not upload highly sensitive portraits.

**What format is exported?**  
Typically a transparent PNG for easy compositing.
